How to Play

Survive until dawn: fifteen minutes. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag anywhere on a phone. Your weapons act on their own.

Gather the glowing orbs to level up, and choose one of three improvements.

Mind your Etiquette. Holding your ground and keeping to the lamplight are proper; constant running and lurking in the dark are not. Genteel weapons favour good manners, Ruffian weapons bad ones.

Install the Game

Eternity is best kept as an app: it opens full screen and sideways, with no browser in the way, and loads at once next time, even without a signal.

On an iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
